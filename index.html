Do I need a whole new website?

Not necessarily. We start with what you have and look for targeted improvements. If a larger rebuild makes more sense, we’ll explain why. A rebuild isn’t silently included in the monthly plan; its scope and cost would need a separate agreement.

Will my website fit the $10 plan?

The plan is for supported small brochure and service-business sites. Your platform, page needs, and integrations determine whether we can host and maintain it within that scope. We can review a public site before knowing the answer, but we won’t promise a migration until we’ve checked the setup.

What counts as a small improvement?

Think lighter images, a clearer service description, a more usable mobile layout, or a simpler contact path. We prioritize and agree the work based on your site’s needs and the effort involved. New backend systems, e-commerce features, and larger redesigns need separately agreed scope—not an assumption that everything fits into $10.

What if I don’t have analytics?

We can still review loading speed, mobile layouts, and public contact paths. Monthly reports distinguish measured results from checks and missing data. Traffic and inquiry reporting depends on available, verified tracking. Any new analytics or booking integration needs an agreed setup, appropriate access, and privacy and consent considerations.

Can you guarantee more customers or a perfect score?

No. Your offer, audience, traffic, and other factors affect results. We focus on removing obstacles and checking the work. PageSpeed scores can vary; a faster page or a button click does not, by itself, prove more inquiries, revenue, or better rankings.

Just curious about speed? Try a self-check.

Google PageSpeed Insights checks a public page’s loading and responsiveness. It’s a useful starting point, not a review of your customer journey.
